Dodge — every map on the site, and you can't catch any of them

Hover a map: it dissolves and rebuilds elsewhere — as a different map from the family. Maps are rasterised and grid-sliced the way DotMap does it, so all ten dissolve at the same granularity no matter how they were drawn. One assemble, but every dodge rolls its own values, so it never repeats without ever changing character. Placement is collision-tested against the other map and the wordmark.
